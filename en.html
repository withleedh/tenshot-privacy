3. Ads, analytics and your choices

Ads · Google AdMob

Google AdMob provides ads such as banners and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Google may process IP addresses, advertising/device identifiers and ad interaction information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent invalid activity. An IP address may be used to estimate your general location.

The app currently requests non-personalized ads. These ads may still involve information needed for delivery, frequency management and fraud prevention. Depending on the applicable region and device settings, a consent or privacy choices screen may be provided. Ads are requested when the required consent and permission to request ads have been confirmed.

To verify rewards, we send Google a random game account identifier and an ad request identifier. Our server checks Google's verification result and transaction identifier to prevent duplicate rewards. Your nickname and support email address are not sent as part of this reward verification.

Usage analytics · Google Analytics for Firebase

Optional usage analytics is off by default. For users who consent, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to analyze game activity and ad-related events. Withdrawing consent in the game settings stops subsequent optional analytics collection and resets analytics data on the device. This does not immediately delete every record already transmitted to a server. You can contact us below to exercise your rights regarding existing records.

We do not set your game account identifier as the Google Analytics user ID. We do not treat analytics consent as consent to personalize ads.

In-app purchases and error diagnostics

In-app purchases use a purchase identifier linked to your game account, product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, purchase, refund and restoration records, and app/device information. Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment; TENSHOT does not collect complete payment card numbers. RevenueCat supports purchase verification, delivery, restoration, refund handling and purchase statistics. Our game server also manages membership periods and reward claims. Test purchase records are separated from production purchases.

Versions with error diagnostics enabled send limited error/crash records, app version, operating system and diagnostic request/match identifiers to Sentry to investigate failures. App screenshots, view hierarchies and default personal-information transmission are disabled. Purchase processing and error diagnostics are service operations separate from optional Firebase usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app and web game use device storage to retain account access, progress, settings and consent choices. You can manage advertising identifier choices in your Android or iOS privacy/advertising settings. Clearing storage or deleting the app may cause you to lose access to a guest account. These actions alone do not delete the account on our server.

Further information: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner services, and Firebase privacy information.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Account, profile, progress and game transaction records: Retained while the account is maintained and the service is provided. Deleting the app does not automatically delete a guest account. Once an account deletion request is verified, the related personal information and linked game data are included in the deletion process.
  • Ad reward verification records: Retained as needed to settle rewards, prevent duplicate grants and manage the account's game transaction records. These records are reviewed and handled together with account deletion.
  • Connection, security and error information: Retained as needed to handle connections, investigate errors or address security issues. Once the purpose is complete, it is deleted or processed so that it no longer identifies an individual.
  • Purchase, restoration, refund and diagnostic records: Retained as needed to provide or restore products, prevent duplicate grants, investigate errors and meet legal obligations. Account deletion requests also include a review of linked service-provider records. Records required by law are deleted once the obligation ends.
  • Support information: Retained as needed to handle inquiries and rights requests. If a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires further retention, only the necessary information is kept until that reason is resolved.
  • Information processed by Google: Ad and analytics information is handled under the relevant service's retention/deletion policies and applicable settings. Firebase installation identifiers may be retained until deletion is requested. Firebase states that removing data from live and backup systems may take up to 180 days after the request. See Firebase retention information and Google retention information.

When personal information is no longer needed, we delete it using methods that make recovery difficult or process it so that it cannot be linked to an individual. If a legal retention requirement or an actual ongoing dispute requires us to retain some information, we explain the reason, the information concerned and when that retention will end. Personal information in backups is also included in the scope of a deletion request. If a backup is restored, the deletion status is applied to prevent a deleted account from being reactivated.

6. Your rights and account deletion requests

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restriction of processing, withdrawal of consent and account deletion. Requests through a representative are also possible under applicable law. A legal guardian may submit a request concerning a child under 14.

TENSHOT privacy and account deletion contact

To request deletion, use “TENSHOT account deletion request” as the email subject. Include your game nickname, platform and request details. We may explain what minimum additional information is needed to verify your account safely.

If applicable law limits a request or processing is delayed, we explain the reason.

You can also seek advice or report privacy concerns to Korea's Privacy Infringement Report Center or Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

7. Information protection measures

TENSHOT encrypts service communications and restricts server access. Mobile account credentials use protected device storage, and the server verifies authentication information using hashes. Game rewards are verified by the server. We limit the information recorded in logs to what is needed for operation.
